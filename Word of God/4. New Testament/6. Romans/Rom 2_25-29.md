> [!bible]+ [Romans 2:25-29 - ESV](https://bolls.life/ESV/45/2/)
> 25. For circumcision indeed is of value if you obey the law, but if you break the law, your circumcision becomes uncircumcision.
> 26. So, if a man who is uncircumcised keeps the precepts of the law, will not his uncircumcision be regarded  as circumcision?
> 27. Then he who is physically  uncircumcised but keeps the law will condemn you who have the written code  and circumcision but break the law.
> 28. For no one is a Jew who is merely one outwardly, nor is circumcision outward and physical.
> 29. But a Jew is one inwardly, and circumcision is a matter of the heart, by the Spirit, not by the letter. His praise is not from man but from God.

My reflections:
A jew is not one who does outward actions but rather by the state of his internal "actions", his heart!

Therefore, someone who does the outward act of circumcision is still not a "jew" unless his heart is also circumcised! And circumcision DOES NOT happen by man's decision to follow the letter of the law (29), but by God's Spirit!! And therefore, the praise shouldn't go to congratulating the man who decided to be circumcised, but to God who did the circumcision of the heart!

Replace circumcision with baptism and you start to see a lot of similarities. Help us God to understand!
