It's the NT continuation of the OT's [[Passover]] - to point us to the Lamb of God! Important to recognize what happened at the institution of it! It's not what many of us from COC heritage intuitively understand what the Lord's Supper to be - a necessary ritual that we MUST partake of. Christians SHOULD and OUGHT to desire to partake it.

**Summary**
1. A continuation of the OT's [[Passover]]. Jews remember God's deliverance and now Christians remember Jesus's deliverance. (Luk 22, )
2. Bread
	1. symbolizes body, for remembrance of Jesus. ([[Luk 22_19]])
	2. "Take, eat; this is my body" -> Called to eat it with understanding that this is Jesus's body ([[Mat 26_26]], [[Mark 14_22]])
3. Fruit of the Vine/Cup
	1. 'poured out' -> symbolize the sacrifice and atonement of Jesus' life for our sins ([[Luk 22_20]], [[Mat 26_28]])
	2. 'new covenant in my blood' -> Jesus's death kickstarts the start of a new covenant (Luk 22_20)
	3. "Drink of it, all of you, for this is my blood of the covenant..." ([[Mat 26_27-28]], [[Mark 14_23-24]]) -> Called to drink understanding this represents the new blood, Christ's, that can finally take away the sins of the world once for all ([[Heb 9_28]], [[1 Pet 3_18]]), the better covenant ([[Heb 7_22]])
	4. "I will not drink again of the fruit of the vine until that day when I drink it new in the kingdom of God" (All the Gospels)-> Thus, the last supper until Jesus's returns and establishes fully the kingdom of God.
4. "For as often as you eat this bread and drink the cup, you **proclaim the Lord 's death until he comes.**" ([[1 Cor 11_26]]) -> Partaking of these 2 items not only for remembrance but for the proclamation of Jesus's death until he comes. I think "Jesus's death until he comes" encompasses the entirety of the Gospel. Who, Why, How, When, What, of it.


Point 2.1, 3.1-2: In [[Luke 22_15-20]], (ESV)
>15 And he said to them, "I have earnestly desired to eat this Passover with you before I suffer. 16 For I tell you I will not eat it until it is fulfilled in the kingdom of God." 17 And he took a cup, and when he had given thanks he said, "Take this, and divide it among yourselves. 18 For I tell you that from now on I will not drink of the fruit of the vine until the kingdom of God comes." 19 And he took **bread**, and when he had given thanks, he broke it and gave it to them, saying, "This is **my body**, which is **given for you**. **Do this in remembrance of me**." 20 And likewise the cup after they had eaten, saying, "This **cup** that is **poured out for you** is the **new covenant in my blood**.

we see the disciples preparing for the [[Passover]]. And as they were eating it, Jesus tells them what the cup and bread is supposed to point to, what they signified, Jesus's body given and blood poured. Meaning as they continued their [[Passover]] year after year, they were to remember what it signified and pointed to - Christ!
